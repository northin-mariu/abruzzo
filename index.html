You're set back from the shore and about 100 m above it, roughly midway between the hilltop borgo and the Foce/Vallevò coast. Nothing is walkable in the nip-out-for-a-beer sense — the sea is a 20-minute walk down and a stiffer one back — but almost everything on this coast is inside ten minutes by car.
